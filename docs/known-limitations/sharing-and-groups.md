# Sharing and groups

Sharing notes with people, and groups, their roles and deleting them. Part of [Known limitations](../known-limitations.md).

- **Email suggestions show the emails of everyone you share a group with.** The add-member and share forms suggest people as you type (see [Sharing](../sharing.md#the-three-access-levels)), drawn from your groups' members and the people you've shared notes with. Those emails were already visible to you — any member can open a group's member list — so the suggestions don't reveal anyone new, but they make it quicker to look through: in a large group, every member's email is a few keystrokes away. Disabled accounts aren't suggested, but can still be added by typing their full email. The lookup is limited to 60 per minute per user — typing many addresses into a group's **Members** box can reach it, and suggestions then stop until the minute is up, without a message — and like the other in-memory limits, that resets on restart.
- **Adding someone to a group, or sharing a note with them, tells you whether their email has an account.** `PUT /api/groups/:id/members` and `PUT /api/notes/:id/shares` answer `404 user_not_found` when no account has that email, and neither has a per-person or per-IP limit. Anyone signed in can create a group and add people to it, so anyone signed in can check as many emails as they like this way; adding several people at once on a group's **Members** page shows **No account with this email** for each such address, with no limit on how many at a time. It isn't silent: an email that does have an account is really added to the group (or the note is really shared with it), and that person — unless their account is disabled, since disabled accounts can be added too — sees the group in their sidebar (or the note under **Shared with me**).
- **Group notes can't be shared with individual people.** Access to a group note comes through the group: the Share dialog of a group note has no per-person sharing, and the per-person sharing endpoints answer `403` on a group note you can read. To let someone in, add them to the group — which also gives them every other note in it (#103, #175).
- **MCP clients don't list your groups.** `list_notes` and `search_notes` include the notes of every group you belong to, and each result's `owner` names the group, with its id; `edit_note` works on a group note when your role in that group can edit, and `create_note` with `group_id` creates a note in a group when your role there can create notes. No MCP tool lists your groups as such. `list_folders` lists the folders of every group whose notes your role can read, with the group's id and name in each folder's `space`, so a group that has folders shows up there even before it has notes; for a group with neither notes nor folders yet, you need its id from a signed-in browser session: it is in the address of the group's panel in the web app (the group's `⋮` menu in the sidebar → **Members & settings**, or **View members** if your role can't manage members), `/groups/<group id>`, and in what `GET /api/groups` returns. The REST move and copy endpoints still accept only a signed-in browser session; a token reaches the same operations through MCP's `move_note_to_group` and `copy_note` (#103, #175).
- **A group's last admin can't leave it or step down.** A group always keeps at least one admin: leaving, being removed, or being demoted as the only admin answers `409 last_admin`. Make someone else an admin first, or delete the group (its notes go to an admin you pick — you, if you're the only one — or are deleted with it) (#103, #175).
- **Disabling a group's only admin leaves the group without a working admin.** Disabling an account doesn't touch its group memberships, so unless another member's role can manage members (which lets them make anyone, themselves included, an Admin) or manage roles and the group (which lets them give their own role Manage members), nobody left in the group can rename it, manage its members or delete it. A site admin can fix it through the API — from a signed-in browser session, since the group endpoints don't accept API tokens, and with the group's id, which has to come from elsewhere because `GET /api/groups` lists only a site admin's own groups (a remaining member sees it in the address of the group's panel, `/groups/<group id>`, or of any of its notes, `/g/<group id>/…`; failing that, the database): promote another member to the group's Admin role (`PATCH /api/groups/:id/members/:userId` with `{roleId}` set to that role's id, from `GET /api/groups/:id/roles`), or, if nobody else is left, add someone with the group's Admin role (`PUT /api/groups/:id/members` with the Admin role's `roleId`) (#103, #175).
- **Site admins can manage a group they don't belong to only through the API.** On the group endpoints a site admin can manage every group — whether or not they're a member, and whatever their role in it is — but they still can't open its notes without being a member, the sidebar and **Settings → Groups** only show groups you're a member of, and opening another group's panel (`/groups/<id>`) shows a not-found message. Using the API takes a signed-in browser session (the group endpoints don't accept API tokens) and the group's id, which `GET /api/groups` won't list for a group you aren't in (#103).
- **The permissions that manage a group are a convenience, not a security boundary.** Someone whose role can manage members can make anyone — themselves included — an Admin, and someone whose role can manage roles and the group can give their own role Manage members. A role meant to "only rename the group" or "only add people" can't stop its holder from taking the rest; give either permission only to people you would trust as an Admin (see [Sharing](../sharing.md#notes-in-a-group)) (#175).
- **After someone's role or its permissions change, pages they already have open may update only the editor; menus and buttons can lag until the page reloads or its tab regains focus.** The note's content, its title and the connection badge follow the role the live-editing connection reports, so they turn read-only, or editable again, within a few seconds. The rest of the page — the **Delete** item in the `⋮` menu, the **Public link** switch in the Share dialog, the **+** next to the group's name in the sidebar — shows what that browser last loaded (the note's own controls also refresh when someone else's edit to the note arrives); only the browser of the person who made the change reloads it right away. Until the holder's page catches up, a control that is still showing can be refused by the server, and one they have just been given stays hidden. Reloading the page, or switching to another tab and back, brings it up to date. The groups offered under **Move to…** and **Copy to…** in a note's `⋮` menu lag the same way: the list is reloaded each time the menu opens, so it is out of date only while the menu stays open (and for a moment after it opens, while it still shows the list loaded earlier); moving or copying into a group where your role can no longer create notes is refused with "We couldn't find that group." This applies to any role change — changing someone's role on the group's member list as well as changing a role's permissions on the **Roles** page of the group's panel (#175).
- **On a group note, two people who can manage its public link can undo each other's link without noticing.** The Share dialog's **Public link** switch shows the state your browser last loaded. If someone else turned the link on after that and you then switch it on too, a new link is made and the one they may already have handed out stops working — switching the link on always makes a new one, the same as **Regenerate link** (#175).
- **If your role can't manage a group note's public link, the Share button and dialog don't tell you the note is public.** They show only the group's access, even when the note also has an anonymous public link; only people whose role can manage public links see it (#175).
- **Leaving a group while one of its notes is open doesn't close that note right away.** Your access ends immediately (see [Sharing](../sharing.md#notes-in-a-group)), but the open page only notices when its live-editing connection re-checks your access — which leaving triggers right away, and which takes at most a few seconds — and then says you no longer have access and takes you back to the home page — the same thing that happens when someone else removes you from the group. ⚠ Derived from reading the code; the case where you leave yourself is not confirmed end to end (#175).
- **Deleting a group with Delete everything can remove a note added to it a moment earlier without telling the people who have it open that it was deleted.** Before anything is deleted, everyone who has one of the group's notes open is taken out of it and told the note has been deleted. A note that is created, moved or copied into the group after that step, but before the deletion itself starts, is deleted along with the rest — but someone who has it open is told "You no longer have access to this note." within a few seconds instead, and what they type in those seconds isn't saved (#175).
- **When deleting a group with Delete everything fails, people who had its notes open may already have been told a note was deleted when it wasn't.** Before anything is deleted, everyone who has one of the group's notes open is taken out of it and told it has been deleted; if the deletion then fails — it was cancelled with "The server is busy" (see below), it ran into some other error, or another admin gave the group's notes to an admin a moment earlier — nothing is deleted, but those people have already seen that message. When it simply failed, the notes are still in the group and can be opened again (reloading the page is enough). When another admin gave the notes away first, they are now that admin's personal notes: the admin who received them can open them again, and everyone else has lost access to them either way (#175).
- **A request a former member had already sent when the group was deleted can still take effect on a note that now belongs to the admin who received it.** Whether a request is allowed is decided from the sender's role in the group when the request arrives, and the write doesn't check again who owns the note. So if the group is deleted with **Give them to an admin** while such a request is in flight, it can still go through on what is now the admin's personal note: deleting the note, editing it through the API or the MCP `edit_note` tool, writing the content of a note they had just created in the group (`POST /api/notes` with `groupId` and `content`, or the MCP `create_note` tool with `group_id` and `content`, first creates the note in the group and then writes its content, and if writing it fails, deletes the note again), reverting an AI edit, uploading an attachment to it — or, if the admin has just turned its public link back on, turning it off again or removing a custom public URL they just gave it. And anything a former member types in an editor they already have open, in the few seconds before it closes, is saved to the admin's note — the same as when someone is removed from a group. The sender was allowed to do each of these when they sent it; the window is only between that check and the write (#175).
- **Deleting a group can occasionally fail with "The server is busy"; trying again works.** If the group is deleted at the same moment as the links between two of its notes are being updated (which happens as someone edits them), or as another group is being given to the same admin, the database can find the two changes waiting on each other and cancel one of them. With transfer, the request also answers `409 server_busy` when the receiving admin's storage space stays busy with other additions for more than 5 seconds, or when that admin's folders stay busy with other changes for more than 5 seconds. Either way, the request can also answer `409 server_busy` when the group's own folders stay busy with other changes for more than 5 seconds. When it cancels the deletion, the request answers `409 server_busy`, nothing is deleted or moved, and the dialog stays open so you can try again (with **Delete everything**, people who had one of its notes open have already been told it was deleted — see above). Every note in the group is handled in that one deletion, so the more notes a group has, the longer it takes (#175).
